Copyright. ISO/IEC documents are copyrighted. Do not paste text from a standard. For each unit, record only the clause number and a short summary in your own words. Summaries stay in this browser and appear only in the private coding file; the checklist and crosswalk exports contain clause numbers and your own questions only.
Method (hybrid deductive–inductive coding, Fereday & Muir-Cochrane 2006). The current checklist is the codebook. For each text unit of another standard, choose Match (an existing item already covers it), Refine (it clarifies an existing item — write the improved question), New (a bias source not in the codebook — write a new question) or Exclude (it fails an inclusion criterion). Keep a unit only if it describes a source or condition of bias, is observable in the system or its data, is in scope for classification or regression decision systems, and is not a mitigation action. A second coder codes the same units independently; the page reports Cohen's κ and the disagreements to resolve.
